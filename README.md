## Research profile

Computational materials scientist specializing in the **surface chemistry of colloidal semiconductor nanocrystals**. I use classical molecular dynamics, free-energy methods and density functional theory to determine how ligands and interfaces control nanocrystal stability, and I develop open tools for building atomistic quantum dot models.

- **6** peer-reviewed publications with international experimental collaborators, plus **1** manuscript in preparation
- PhD defense expected in **March 2027**

## Technical expertise

| Area | What I do |
| --- | --- |
| **Classical MD** (GROMACS) | Ligand-capped nanocrystal models and nucleation of PbSe nanocrystals; the classical MD in all my co-authored papers |
| **Free-energy methods** | Alchemical free-energy calculations and metadynamics, applied to ligand binding on CsPbBr<sub>3</sub> nanocrystal surfaces |
| **DFT** (CP2K) | Geometry optimisation, single-point calculations and ab initio molecular dynamics (AIMD) of quantum dot models |
| **Atomistic model building** | Quantum dots from Wulff constructions with charge-balanced ligand passivation; curation of structure databases |
| **Python** | Trajectory analysis with MDAnalysis, nanocrystal force-field tools with Auto-FOX, and my own set-up scripts ([CompTools](https://github.com/samadel98/CompTools)) |
| **HPC** | Large simulation campaigns on HPC clusters |

## Experience

**Pre-doctoral Researcher** · *Mar 2023 – present*<br>
[BCMaterials](https://www.bcmaterials.net/en/people) – Basque Center for Materials, Applications and Nanostructures, Spain · Computational Materials Science group

- **Ligand binding:** computed ligand binding free energies on CsPbBr<sub>3</sub> nanocrystal surfaces with alchemical free-energy calculations and metadynamics, to assess how strongly different ligands passivate the surface (poster, Beilstein Symposium 2023).
- **Nucleation:** simulated the nucleation of PbSe nanocrystals with classical force fields (poster, MATSUS Spring 2025).
- **Core–shell interfaces:** performed part of the calculations on interfacial reconstructions in III–V@II–VI core–shell quantum dots (second author, *ACS Energy Lett.* 2026).
- **Support for experiments:** performed the classical MD simulations for experimental partners studying perovskite nanocomposite scintillators, ligand exchange for light-emitting diodes, olefin ligand metathesis and perovskite degradation (five co-authored articles, 2025–2026).

**Master's Research Intern** · *Mar 2022 – Aug 2022*<br>
University of Córdoba (UCO), Spain

- Studied cation transfer in sodium and magnesium battery materials with DFT for the Master's thesis *"DFT Calculations for Cation Transfer within Na and Mg Batteries"*.

### Research projects

- **IKUR HPC-IA Project** — *Modelling of colloidal semiconductor quantum dots by classical molecular dynamics.* Responsible for building atomistic models, running force-field simulations and analysing surface- and ligand-related phenomena.
- **UNICORN Project – Hybrid Nanocomposite Scintillators** — *Computational design of quantum dots for next-generation scintillators.* Modelled PbSe quantum dots with MD and DFT, analysed ligand and surface effects, and built a quantum dot structure database.
